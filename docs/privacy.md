# Privacy statement

Scattergories saves your settings on this device. It has no accounts, cookies, advertising or analytics. Your categories and settings are not sent to us.

This page covers [scattergories.duinlab.nl](https://scattergories.duinlab.nl). Simon van Lierde runs this unofficial companion for the Hasbro game.

## What Scattergories stores on your device

Your browser keeps:

- your language, theme and sound preferences;
- the round length, get-ready time, category count and repeat-letter preference;
- your category deck, custom categories, pinned categories and whether the category panel is open;
- whether you have finished the welcome screen.

Settings are saved automatically, including default settings on your first visit. The app also caches its files so it can work offline. Fonts ship with the app.

Players write answers on paper. The app does not record answers, scores or player names.

## How to delete it

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings. This removes saved preferences, custom categories and offline files. They cannot be recovered from us.

## What our host sees

[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) hosts the site. When you load it online, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details to serve the files. Its privacy policy explains how it handles that information.

The app has no application server and keeps no logs of your play. All app files load from this site's address. Links to the source code, official game and other websites open those sites only when you follow them.

## Cookies

Scattergories sets no cookies. Browser storage keeps the preferences above and the files needed to work offline.

## Your rights

We cannot read or change the settings kept in your browser. For questions about personal data or this statement, email [code@duinlab.nl](mailto:code@duinlab.nl).

You can ask about access, correction or deletion of personal data we hold. You can also complain to your data protection authority, including the Dutch [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).
